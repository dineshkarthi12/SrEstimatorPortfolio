I'm a results-driven Senior Estimator and Quantity Surveyor in Chennai, with 7+ years in quantity take-off, BOM and BOQ preparation, rate analysis and cost control for commercial and residential interior fit-out, joinery, civil finishing and MEP packages.

Sheet A-03 Process Six steps · left to right

How a drawing becomes a number.

The steps overlap on a live job. This is the order I keep.

  1. 01 → 02

    Step 1: Drawing & spec study

    Read every sheet, legend and specification before measuring anything. Note what is missing and raise RFIs early.

    • AutoCAD 2D
    • Specifications
    • Legends
    • RFIs
  2. 02 → 03

    Step 2: Quantity take-off

    Measure each item to IS 1200, so anyone using the same rules gets the same quantity.

    • IS 1200
    • AutoCAD 2D
    • Excel take-off sheets
  3. 03 → 04

    Step 3: BOM preparation

    Turn quantities into materials: sizes, units and wastage allowances a buyer can order from.

    • MTO
    • Wastage allowances
    • Excel
  4. 04 → 05

    Step 4: BOQ preparation

    Group items by trade with clear descriptions, units and quantities, ready for pricing.

    • Trade-wise BOQ
    • Item descriptions
    • Units
  5. 05 → 06

    Step 5: Vendor quotes & rate analysis

    Compare vendor quotes and build each unit rate from material, labour and overheads.

    • Comparative statements
    • CPWD DSR/SOR
    • Market rates
  6. 06 End

    Step 6: Final costing & submission

    Summarise, check every total, issue the revision and track each change until sign-off.

    • Cost summary
    • Revisions
    • Variation orders
